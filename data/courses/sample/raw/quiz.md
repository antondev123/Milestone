# Existing quiz

## Section 1: Budgeting
1. In the 50/30/20 rule, what does the 20 represent? (Answer: saving and/or paying off debt)
2. Why should a budget include a "surprises" line? (Open. Model answer: because real months have unplanned costs, and having a place to take that money from stops it coming out of essentials like rent.)
3. What should you do for one month before changing your spending? (Answer: track actual transactions using your bank history, without guessing)

## Section 2: Interest
1. R1000 at 10% simple interest for 3 years gives you how much? (Answer: R1300)
2. Explain in your own words why compound interest grows faster than simple interest. (Open. Model answer: because each period's interest is calculated on the original amount plus the interest already earned, so the base keeps growing.)
3. You have R5000 in savings earning 5% and a store card charging 22%. Which should you use the money for and why? (Open. Model answer: pay the store card, because the debt costs more than the savings earn.)

## Section 3: Credit score
1. Name one thing that improves your credit score. (Answer: paying accounts on time / keeping card balance under ~30% of limit / long history of good accounts)
2. How many free credit reports are you entitled to from each bureau per year? (Answer: one)
3. Why does applying for a lot of credit in a short time hurt your score? (Open. Model answer: each application is recorded and many applications signal risk to lenders.)

## Section 4: Tax
1. What does PAYE stand for? (Answer: pay as you earn)
2. True or false: if a raise pushes you into a higher bracket, all your income is taxed at the higher rate. (Answer: false, only the income above the bracket line)
3. Name one thing that reduces the tax you owe. (Answer: retirement annuity / pension contributions, medical aid credit, tax-free savings account)
