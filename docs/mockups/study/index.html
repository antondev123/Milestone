Study mode: three directions

Hands-on, not commuting. The book as it is, read-aloud with the spoken words tracked, an assistant to ask, and a normal tap/type checkpoint. Separate from Listen mode; the place carries over. Each mockup is interactive: press play, toggle audio off, open the assistant, answer the questions.

A. Reader with a now-reading strip

Reflowed page in Carry type. Word highlight on the page, current sentence mirrored in a pinned mini-player at the bottom. Assistant as a bottom sheet. Checkpoint inline at the end of the passage.

B. The page as printed, assistant beside it

White PDF-style page cards with the LibreTexts look. Highlighter-pen overlay for word and sentence. Floating control pill. Assistant is an edge drawer on the phone and a persistent right column on tablet. Checkpoint slides up as a sheet.

Open B

C. Teleprompter ticker, assistant anchored to sentences

Sticky ink strip under the top bar where the current sentence fills in word by word. The page stays quiet (gold rule only). Questions are inline cards pinned to the sentence they were asked about; collapse to a margin dot. Concept check in the book's own style.
